## About Me

I am a lecturer at [Shenzhen Polytechnic University](https://english.szpu.edu.cn/), where my research and teaching focus on GUI world models and educational agents.

I received my Ph.D. from [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/), advised by [Prof. Yui-Lam Chan](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/eee/people/academic-staff-and-teaching-staff/prof-chan-yui-lam/). I subsequently worked there as a postdoctoral researcher. I received my master's degree from Guangdong University of Technology, advised by [Prof. Wing-Kuen Ling](https://english.gdut.edu.cn/info/1025/1108.htm), an IET Fellow, a Guangdong Pearl River Scholar, and a recipient of China's Thousand Young Talents Program.

## Research Interests

- **GUI World Models:** Modeling graphical user interfaces and predicting how they change in response to actions.
- **Educational Agents:** Learning assistance, hands-on instruction, and feedback during practice.
- **Screen Content Video Enhancement:** Restoring text and graphics in compressed screen videos through spatiotemporal feature learning.
